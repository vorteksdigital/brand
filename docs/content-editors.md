# Editor guide

Sign in at `/admin`. Create Media first; describe meaningful images, or mark
purely decorative images and leave alt blank. Create Pages and Posts with stable
slugs. `home` is the homepage. Save Draft keeps work private; Preview opens
authenticated draft mode; Publish makes it public. Live Preview refreshes during
autosave and supports mobile, tablet, and desktop widths.

Posts need title, content, author/category where applicable, publication date,
and a concise SEO title/description. Related Posts are manually curated and
cannot include the current post. Before publishing, check links, heading order,
image alternatives, preview widths, social preview, and factual freshness.
